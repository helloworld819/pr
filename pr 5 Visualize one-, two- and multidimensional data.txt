dataset - superstore.csv
# One-dimensional data — Sales Distribution by Region
1. Open a new worksheet named Sales by Region.
2. Drag Region to Rows.
3. Drag Sales to Columns.
4. Choose Bar on the Marks card or use Show Me → Horizontal Bars.
5. Drag Sales to Label.
6. Sort the bars in descending order of sales. go to region drop down then sort by field -> descending
7. Set the title to Sales Distribution by Region.

#Two-dimensional data — Regional Sales Breakdown by Segment
1. Open a new worksheet named Regional Sales by Segment.
2. Drag Region to Rows.
3. Drag Sales to Columns.
4. Select Bar as the mark type.
5. Drag Segment to Color.
6. Ensure Analysis → Stack Marks → On is enabled.
7. Drag Sales to Label if needed.
8. Set the title to Regional Sales Breakdown by Segment.

#Multidimensional data — Sales by Region, Segment and Category
1. Open a new worksheet named Multidimensional Sales Analysis.
2. Drag Region to Columns.
3. Drag Sales to Rows.
4. Select Bar as the mark type.
5. Drag Segment to Columns, next to Region, if you want separate bars for each segment within each region.
6. Drag Category to Color.
7. Ensure stacked marks are enabled if you want the category breakdown represented by colored sections.
8. Optionally drag Sales to Label.
9. Set the title to Sales Analysis by Region and Category with Segment Breakdown.
