1. Connect to weatherhistory.csv.
2. Open a new worksheet named Monthly Temperature Trends.
3. Check the field names in the Data pane. The PDF describes a date field and a temperature field; the example dataset commonly uses Formatted Date and Temperature (C).
4. If Formatted Date is not recognized as a date, change its data type to Date & Time.
5. Drag Formatted Date to Columns.
6. Click its drop-down arrow and select Month. To show the seasonal pattern across the entire date range, use the continuous month/date timeline rather than grouping all Januaries together.
7. Drag Temperature (C) to Rows.
8. Change the aggregation to Average if it is not already AVG(Temperature (C)).
9. Select Line from the Marks card.
10. Open the Analytics pane.
11. Drag Trend Line onto the view and select a linear trendline.
12. Set the title to Monthly Temperature Trends.
