
1. Connect to the same Superstore dataset.
2. Open a new worksheet and rename it Quarterly Sales Performance.
3. Drag Order Date to Columns.
4. Click the drop-down arrow on the Order Date pill.
5. Select Quarter under the date options. Use the discrete quarter level if you want separate quarterly marks. and go to quarter then custom then select date value.
6. Drag Sales to Rows. It should display SUM(Sales).
7. On the Marks card, select Line.
8. Drag Sales to Label if you want values displayed at each point.
9. Ensure the quarters are arranged chronologically from 2011 to 2014.
10. Set the title to Quarterly Sales Performance
